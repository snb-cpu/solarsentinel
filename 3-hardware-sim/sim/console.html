Connecting to the simulator…

Each cell shows its real temperature in the simulator, coloured against the panel's median (blue cooler, red hotter). The tan film is dust and the white patch is a bird dropping, both placed by the simulator. The four sun dots are the corner light sensors; they turn blue when the light is uneven.

The electronics

Everything the ESP32 reads goes through two multiplexers into one 16-bit ADC, so the ESP32's own noisy ADC is never used. Sensors on the 5 V rail switch off while it sleeps.
